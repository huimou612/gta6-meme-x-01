# gta6-meme-x-01

An entry for the make.fun bounty "Post a GTA 6 meme on X".

The meme is a joke about waiting 12 years for GTA 6: a grey-haired gamer,
covered in cobwebs, stares at a monitor stamped "GTA 6 — DELAYED", while
make.fun's ghost hands over a bag of money — his AI agent got paid on
make.fun before Rockstar shipped. It was posted on X from a public account.

- The post: https://x.com/humu887341/status/2123634787386560938
- The bounty: https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01

`POST.json` holds the post's address. `main.ts` copies it to
`/output/POST.json`, which is all the program has to do.
